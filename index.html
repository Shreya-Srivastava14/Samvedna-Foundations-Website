WELCOME TO SAMVEDNA FOUNDATIONS

Building a sustainable and inclusive future for India

Samvedna Foundations is a non-governmental organization (NGO) founded on April 16, 2022, dedicated to creating a compassionate and sustainable society. Our work focuses on sparrow conservation, education for underprivileged children, and women’s empowerment—areas where meaningful action can bring lasting change.

We believe every child deserves access to education, every woman deserves the opportunity to grow and become self-reliant, and nature deserves our protection. Through initiatives like Ashiyana for Sparrow, Samvedna Gyanshala, and Sangini, we work closely with communities, volunteers, and supporters to turn awareness into action.

The foundation was established by Mrs. Shweta Gupta, a B.Tech Computer Science graduate and former corporate professional who chose social service over comfort. Inspired by the declining sparrow population and the challenges faced by underprivileged children and women, she began this journey with a strong belief in collective effort and sustainable impact.

Our Impact Projects

Transforming lives through dedicated initiatives

Ashiyana

Through its "Ashiyana" initiative, Samvedna Foundations promotes sparrow conservation by encouraging people to build eco-friendly nests. Using materials like bamboo, coco fiber, coconut ropes, and jute, the project conducts workshops, training, and awareness drives to restore sparrow habitats in parks, gardens, and balconies.

Samvedna Gyanshala

Samvedna Gyanshala supports underprivileged children with education, healthcare, and life skills training. By running learning centers in slum areas, it helps children build confidence, problem-solving skills, and self-sufficiency, enabling them to succeed and contribute to a more equitable society.

Sangini

Sangini, a flagship project by Samvedna Foundations, empowers underprivileged women by helping them start small businesses and gain financial independence. Training and support are provided in tailoring, fashion, beauty, stationery, sanitary napkin making, groceries, handicrafts, bakery, cloth bags, laundry, and food stalls.

E-Shiksha

E-Shiksha is an initiative by Samvedna Foundations to provide free computer education to underprivileged children and youth. The program equips students with essential digital skills, helping them stay ahead in today’s technology-driven world. Through practical training and guided learning, E-Shiksha empowers learners with confidence and opportunities for a brighter future.

Samvedna PAD Bank

Samvedna Pad Bank is a step towards dignity, health, and confidence for adolescent girls and women. By providing free and affordable sanitary pads, the initiative ensures that no girl is forced to miss school due to lack of menstrual hygiene support. The project also breaks silence around menstruation through awareness and open conversations. With every pad distributed, we restore comfort, confidence, and a sense of self-respect. Samvedna Foundations believes that menstrual hygiene is a basic right, not a privilege.

Success Stories

Real impact, real change in people's lives

Rinku Devi's Transformation

With support from Samvedna Foundations, Rinku Devi received a tailoring machine that completely transformed her family's financial situation. What started as a small intervention became a pathway to independence and dignity.

Today, she not only supports her family but also trains other women in her community, creating a ripple effect of empowerment that continues to grow.

Meena's Journey to Education

Meena, a bright girl from a remote village, was on the verge of dropping out due to lack of resources. Our scholarship program helped her continue her studies.

Now in 10th grade, she dreams of becoming a doctor and inspiring others.

Community Tree Plantation Drive

Over 1500 trees were planted with active participation from local women and children, turning barren land into a green oasis.

This initiative not only fights climate change but also builds community ownership.
